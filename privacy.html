06

Contact communications

If you contact us by email, we receive the email address used to send the message and the information contained in your communication.

We use this information to read and respond to your request, provide support, discuss cooperation, answer legal or business questions and maintain reasonable records of relevant communications.

Depending on the nature of your request, processing may be based on our legitimate interest in handling communications or on steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.

You are not required to provide more information than is reasonably necessary for us to understand and respond to your request.

07

Technical and server-log data

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. When the website is visited, GitHub may automatically log and store a visitor’s IP address for security purposes, regardless of whether the visitor is signed in to GitHub.

GitHub or the underlying hosting infrastructure may also process technical information necessary to deliver website content, maintain security, prevent abuse and diagnose technical problems. This may include the request date and time, requested page, response status, browser information, operating system information and referring page.

DeerDigital does not use this technical data to create advertising profiles or to track visitors across unrelated websites.

08

Cookies and similar technologies

This website does not currently use cookies, local storage, advertising trackers or non-essential analytics technologies.

The website consists primarily of static pages used to present DeerDigital, its divisions, games and mobile applications. Its visual effects, navigation and other basic functionality operate without storing information on a visitor’s device.

As the website is hosted through GitHub Pages, GitHub may automatically process technical information, including a visitor’s IP address, for security, infrastructure and service-operation purposes. This processing is controlled by GitHub and is subject to GitHub’s own privacy documentation.

Because DeerDigital does not currently use non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies on this website, no cookie-consent banner is displayed.

If analytics, advertising, embedded third-party content or other technologies that store or access information on a visitor’s device are introduced in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated and an appropriate consent mechanism will be provided where required by applicable law.

09

Website analytics

DeerDigital does not currently use Google Analytics, advertising analytics, behavioural tracking or similar analytics services on this website.

Basic technical and traffic information may be processed by the website’s hosting provider, GitHub Pages, as part of operating, securing and maintaining the hosting service.

If DeerDigital introduces an analytics service in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or at the time the service is activated. Where legally required, the analytics service will remain disabled until the visitor has provided valid consent.

12

Data retention

Personal data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

Routine enquiries Usually for the time needed to respond and for a reasonable follow-up period
Business communications For the duration of negotiations or the business relationship and relevant limitation periods
Technical logs For a limited period determined by security, hosting and diagnostic requirements
Consent records For as long as reasonably necessary to demonstrate the consent or withdrawal
Legal records For periods required by applicable tax, accounting or other legal obligations

Data may be retained for longer where reasonably necessary for an ongoing dispute, security incident, legal claim or binding legal obligation.

When data is no longer needed, it will be deleted, anonymised or otherwise handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

13

Data security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal data against accidental loss, unauthorised access, misuse, alteration or disclosure.

Measures may include encrypted connections, restricted access to accounts, software updates, secure authentication and use of reputable hosting and communication providers.

No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Visitors should therefore avoid sending unnecessary confidential information through ordinary email.
