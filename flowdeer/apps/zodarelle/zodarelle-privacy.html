03

Privacy summary

  • Zodarelle does not require a proprietary account.
  • Zodarelle does not operate a proprietary backend containing horoscope profiles.
  • Birth-related information and zodiac preferences are stored locally on the device.
  • The operator normally cannot see or remotely recover that local information.
  • The operator may receive information when a person voluntarily contacts support.
  • Google may process information separately in connection with Google Play.
04

Information stored locally

Depending on the current version, the Application may store information such as:

  • selected zodiac sign;
  • date of birth;
  • birth month and day;
  • horoscope preferences;
  • selected content categories;
  • favourites;
  • previously viewed content;
  • notification preferences;
  • language and display preferences;
  • other Application settings.

This information is stored locally on the user's device and is used by the Application to provide personalised horoscope content.

The operator does not normally receive or remotely access this locally stored information.

05

Birth information and zodiac signs

Zodarelle may ask for a date of birth or zodiac sign so that the Application can determine which horoscope or zodiac content should be displayed.

This information is used for Application functionality and horoscope personalisation on the device.

The operator does not require a user's real name in order to provide the core horoscope functionality.

Birth-related information is not used by the operator to verify identity, establish legal age or create an external user profile.

No proprietary account or backend

Zodarelle does not currently require registration using an email address, password or social login.

The operator does not maintain a proprietary database containing individual horoscope profiles.

This means that the operator normally cannot recover local preferences after they have been deleted or the device has been lost.

Advertising and analytics

The current Zodarelle configuration described by this Policy does not rely on a proprietary analytics backend to receive horoscope-profile information.

13

Recipients

Operator-controlled data may be processed by:

  • the operator's email service provider;
  • Google in connection with Google Play;
  • technical providers where reasonably necessary;
  • accounting, tax or legal advisers where required;
  • public authorities where disclosure is legally required.

The operator does not sell personal information.

Locally stored horoscope-profile information is not provided by the operator to advertising data brokers.

International data transfers

Where European data-protection law applies, international transfers must rely on an appropriate legal mechanism such as an adequacy decision, standard contractual clauses or another legally recognised safeguard.

15

Data retention

  • Local horoscope and preference information remains on the device until deleted, cleared or removed with the Application.
  • Support communications may be retained for the time necessary to respond and for a reasonable follow-up period.
  • Purchase, complaint and legal records may be retained for statutory or limitation periods.
  • Data independently controlled by Google is retained according to Google's own rules.
16

Deleting information

Local information can generally be removed by:

  • resetting preferences inside the Application where available;
  • clearing Application storage through Android settings;
  • uninstalling the Application.

Because the operator does not maintain a proprietary Zodarelle user account, the operator normally cannot remotely delete, restore or return information stored only on your device.

17

Your data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • request access;
  • request correction;
  • request deletion;
  • request restriction;
  • request portability;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis;
  • lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

In the Czech Republic, the supervisory authority is the Office for Personal Data Protection — Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů.

These rights generally apply to data processed by the operator. The operator cannot retrieve information that exists exclusively in local Application storage to which the operator has no access.

18

Security

Zodarelle's local-storage design reduces the need to transmit horoscope-profile information to remote servers.

Users should nevertheless protect their device through appropriate security measures, including:

  • screen locking;
  • operating-system updates;
  • preventing unauthorised physical access;
  • installing Applications only from trusted sources.

No technical system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

19

Younger users

Zodarelle is not designed to encourage younger users to disclose identifying information to the operator.

The Application does not require a proprietary account or public profile.

A person who cannot legally accept the Application's Terms independently should use the Application only with the knowledge and permission of a parent or legal guardian.
