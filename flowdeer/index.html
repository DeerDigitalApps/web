Our approach

Less friction.
More value.

01

Clear from the beginning

People should understand an app without tutorials, manuals or endless onboarding screens.

02

Useful before impressive

Design exists to make the product better, not to distract from what the product does.

03

Private by default

Whenever possible, personal information stays on the device and the app works without an account.

04

Enjoyable every day

Useful apps should still feel polished, satisfying and pleasant to return to.
