Kids Games does not ask children for personal information.

The Application has no account system, no advertising, no analytics and no proprietary backend for storing children's gameplay information.

01

Scope

This Privacy Policy applies exclusively to JellyDeer: Kids Games.

Other JellyDeer applications and the DeerDigital website may have their own privacy policies.

03

Privacy summary

  • No JellyDeer user account is required.
  • No proprietary backend stores game progress.
  • No advertising is displayed.
  • No advertising SDK is used.
  • No analytics SDK is used.
  • Game progress and settings are stored locally.
  • Children are not asked to provide their name, email address or contact information.
  • Google Play independently handles installation and purchases.
04

Children's privacy

Kids Games is intended for children and families and is designed to minimise data processing.

The Application does not ask children to submit:

  • their real name;
  • email address;
  • telephone number;
  • postal address;
  • date of birth;
  • photographs or videos;
  • precise location;
  • contacts;
  • microphone recordings.

The Application does not include public profiles, messaging, chat rooms or social networking.

05

Locally stored game information

The Application may store the following information locally on the device:

  • completed levels;
  • current progress;
  • unlocked game content;
  • game preferences;
  • sound settings;
  • visual preferences;
  • other technical game settings.

This information is used only to make the game work correctly on the device.

The operator does not normally receive or remotely access this locally stored game information.

06

Personal data collected by the Application

The Application itself is not designed to collect identifying personal information from children.

In particular, it does not maintain a database associating game progress with a child's identity.

The operator may only receive personal information where an adult voluntarily contacts support or otherwise communicates directly with the operator.

07

No accounts or proprietary backend

Kids Games does not require registration, usernames, passwords or social login.

There is no proprietary JellyDeer server used to store children's profiles, progress or gameplay history.

This also means that the operator normally cannot restore local game progress after it has been deleted or lost.

08

No analytics

Kids Games does not use an analytics SDK to track how individual children use the Application.

The operator does not create behavioural profiles based on gameplay and does not analyse individual children's activity for marketing purposes.

Google Play may independently provide developers with aggregated or technical store information, such as installation or stability information. This does not give the operator access to local gameplay progress.

09

No advertising

Kids Games does not display third-party advertisements.

The Application does not use AdMob or another advertising SDK.

Children's gameplay information is not used for personalised advertising or advertising profiles.

10

Optional level purchases

The Application may offer optional one-time purchases that unlock additional levels or game packs.

Purchases are handled through Google Play Billing.

The operator does not receive complete payment-card information, bank details or Google account credentials.

To provide purchased content, the Application may receive limited purchase information from Google Play, such as:

  • product identifier;
  • purchase status;
  • purchase token;
  • information necessary to confirm whether the content has been purchased.

This information is used only to provide, verify or restore the purchased content.

11

Google Play

Google independently processes information connected with Google Play, including where applicable:

  • Application installation;
  • updates;
  • device compatibility;
  • Google account information;
  • platform security;
  • purchase processing;
  • purchase history.

This processing is governed by Google's own terms, privacy documentation and account settings.

12

Support communication

If a parent or another adult contacts the operator, the operator may receive:

  • the sender's email address;
  • the sender's name if provided;
  • the message content;
  • screenshots or attachments;
  • device and technical details voluntarily supplied for troubleshooting.

This information is used only to respond to the request, investigate an issue or handle a complaint.

Parents should avoid sending unnecessary information about their child.

13

Purposes of processing

Where the operator directly processes personal information, it may be used to:

  • respond to support enquiries;
  • investigate technical problems;
  • handle complaints;
  • verify purchases where necessary;
  • comply with legal obligations;
  • protect legal rights.
14

Legal bases

Legitimate interest in responding to questions and resolving technical issues.

Purchases

Compliance with consumer, accounting, tax or other legal requirements.

Legal claims

Legitimate interest in protecting legal rights and resolving disputes.

15

Recipients

Operator-controlled information may be processed by:

  • the operator's email provider for support communication;
  • Google in connection with Google Play;
  • accounting or legal advisers where necessary;
  • public authorities where legally required.

The operator does not sell children's personal data.

16

International transfers

Google, an email provider or another necessary service provider may process information outside the European Economic Area.

Where European data-protection law applies, appropriate legal transfer mechanisms must be used.

17

Data retention

  • Local game data remains on the device until it is deleted, cleared or the Application is uninstalled.
  • Support communications are retained only as reasonably necessary to respond and follow up.
  • Purchase and accounting records may be kept for legally required periods.
  • Information controlled by Google is retained under Google's own policies.
18

Deleting game data

Locally stored game information can generally be removed by:

  • using a reset option where provided;
  • clearing the Application's storage in Android;
  • uninstalling the Application.

Because there is no proprietary game account or backend, the operator normally cannot remotely access, recover or delete data stored only on the device.

19

Data-protection rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights including:

  • access;
  • correction;
  • deletion;
  • restriction;
  • data portability;
  • objection;
  • withdrawal of consent where consent applies;
  • complaint to a supervisory authority.

In the Czech Republic, the supervisory authority is the Office for Personal Data Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů).

These rights apply to information accessible to the operator. The operator cannot retrieve gameplay information stored exclusively on the device.

20

Security

The Application's local and offline-first design reduces the amount of information transmitted from the device.

No technical system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Parents should use appropriate device protections and Google Play purchase authentication.

21

Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Policy will be updated if Kids Games introduces materially different data practices or services.

In particular, the Policy would need to be updated before introducing:

  • a proprietary account system;
  • a proprietary backend;
  • cloud synchronisation;
  • advertising;
  • analytics;
  • new SDKs that process personal information.
